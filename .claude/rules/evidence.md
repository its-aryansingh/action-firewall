# Evidence and submission rules

- Prefer first-party Razorpay, NPCI, RBI, or official repository sources for current
  product, protocol, regulatory, and integration claims.
- Date time-sensitive findings and label first-party evidence, internal proof,
  secondary reporting, inference, and unconfirmed claims separately.
- Never invent access to Vulcan, private Razorpay APIs, partner pilots, or proprietary
  data.
- Keep payment-link issuance separate from payment, capture, settlement, and revenue.
- Keep UAP and regulatory material in market context unless an operative primary
  specification is attached and implemented.
- When test totals, demo amounts, lifecycle names, or limitations change, update every
  judge-facing artifact before committing.
